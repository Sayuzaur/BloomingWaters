**For beta 1.7.3 and STAPI**!

**BloomingWaters** is a Swamplands expansion. It reworks whole biome, focusing more on idea of colourful wetlands, instead of dark and gloomy modern Minecraft swamps. BloomingWaters adds new wetlands flora, mud surface blocks, new woodset and even original biome-specific music.

![Example image of player base in reworked Swamplands with all new blocks.](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/2327000cd6cac09a3d83816c9b850073a56a1feb.jpeg)

## Main Features

- **Reworked biome generation** - Whole biome got reworked with new generation rules and features. Every single rule/feature can be turned on and off.
- **New music** - 2 new songs, that are not just ports from new Minecraft versions, but brand new, minecraft-style, tracks. With ZastavkaAPI they're set as biome-specific and plays in Swamplands.
- **Weeping Willow** - New tree, Weeping Willow, together with vines and planks set. Brighter and smoother than oak.
- **Water grasses** - Cattails and Reeds grows near or in water. Mainly decorative blocks, but Reeds are also used to craft paper.
- **Lily pads** - Docorative block that can be jumped on to traverse bodies of water. In 2 variants (normal and cold one), also including lily flowers!
- **Duckweed** - Covering bodies of water in green carpet.
- **Flowers** - 3 new flowers, renewable with bonemeal, used to craft dyes, simply beautiful.
- **Mushrooms** - 4 new mushrooms, including one shelf fungus. Can spread on it's own (on mud, near water, dark), or with bonemeal. Has grown stages with different texture.
- **Firefly bush** - Decorative bush with small glowing in dark dots reasembling fireflies.
- **Mud** - Mud and Peat are not only decorative, but also used for bonemealing plants on. Mud and Peat can be dried (either in furnace or by placing them in nether) for new variants. Dried Peat can be used as effective fuel source.
- **Moss** - Moss blocks and carpet, bright green, perfect for decoration. Carpet generates 'overhangs' on every solid block, similar to grass on dirt.
- **Mossy logs** - Just like cobblestone, logs have their mossy variants. Can be crafted with moss.
- **Sinking mud** - Rare mud variant that slows and sinks entities. Instead of being dug with shovel, you need to collect it with a bucket.

## Required dependencies

- **[StationAPI](https://modrinth.com/mod/stationapi)** - version >= **2.0.0-alpha.6.2**

## Recommended mods

- **[ZastavkaAPI](https://modrinth.com/mod/zastavkaapi)** - Required to set new music as biome-specific. Without ZastavkaAPI, new music will play in every biome.
- **[Glass Config API](https://modrinth.com/mod/glass-config-api)** - Required to change default mod config.
- **[BH Creative](https://modrinth.com/mod/bh-creative)** - BloomingWaters has own creative tab with every new item.
- **[Always More Items](https://modrinth.com/mod/always-more-items)** - Recomended to see all the recipes added by BloomingWaters.

## Compatibility

- **No known compatibility issues**. BloomingWaters doesn't use any mixins, doesn't add any new mechanics and has configurable world generation.
- New items uses tags system (like 'c:planks'), so crafting recipes even from other mods should be comaptible if also using tags.
